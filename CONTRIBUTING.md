# Contributing

Suggestions, code improvements, and translation reviews are welcome. Open an issue to discuss substantial work and submit changes through a pull request.

The site currently has no archive articles. Before proposing a source text or translation for publication, record the source URL, rights holder, basis for republication and translation, and any limitations. A link to a publicly accessible work alone does not establish these rights. Do not add material while its rights are unresolved.

For Bulgarian text, preserve meaning and context, use UTF-8, and request review by a fluent Bulgarian speaker. Clearly identify machine-assisted translations and do not mark them reviewed without human review. Keep citations and sources intact. Develop a glossary as articles are approved.

By contributing code, you agree to provide it under the repository's MIT license. Discuss rights and permissions for non-code contributions before submitting them.
